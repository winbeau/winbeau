## 🧭 Research Focus

- **Autoregressive video generation** — turning long-form video synthesis into a tractable AR problem.
- **AR-DiT** — autoregressive Diffusion Transformer backbones; current main line of work.
- **AR-MoT** — autoregressive Mixture-of-Transformers; exploring conditional routing for video tokens.
- **WMA (World-Model-Augmented)** — interested in tying generation tighter to learned world models for consistency and controllability.
